Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis

"While still possible, the proof exploiting a bug is vanishingly small now. My monitor was cutting my download speed by 75%

"This reminds me of a (completely different, but felt equally) bizzare problem with ghostty (the terminal emulator) on Linux and Steam, specifically Baldur's Gate 3.At the start of the game after an update, BG3 compiles the (Vulkan) shaders. To avoid hammering the computer, it checks the load, including I/O pressure, and then throttles itself. It turns out that Ghostty uses io_uring, and even if it's not doing anything this somehow counts as pressure. This was made worse by the fact that I usually have a dozen or so Ghostty windows.End result, BG3 compiles shaders using 1 core, throttled, instead of all available 20 cores. Instead of a minute or so, it'd take several hours.(For closure: I configured ghostty to use epoll instead. Probably not optimal but works around the problem.)"

"Better description: "My USB network switch was cutting my download speed".The fact that it's a monitor is a little bit irrelevant."

"Since lots of comments misunderstood the article, the extra latency from going through the monitor meant his laptop couldn't acknowledge the packets quickly enough. That led a buffer on the ethernet adapter to fill and that led it to drop packets.A different adapter which required less frequent acknowledgement fixed the problem for him.It is quite a nice post which gets to the bottom of incredibly opaque behaviour of USB hubs."
