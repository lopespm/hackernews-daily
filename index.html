GLM-5.4: Toward generalized coding with our first loop transformer

"I love the transparency, or at least given technical details, that Zhipu provide - a refreshing change from the locked-down approach of the western companies, and an interesting glimpse into the direction of SOTA LLMs.Looped Transformers are an obvious direction even if there are still problems to be worked out. The original Transformer is a bit like an unrolled for loop, and rather unnaturally pairs different sets of weights, both for attention and feed forward (knowledge) with different "layers" (aka loop iterations). Far more natural is to strive for something more like our brain's thalamo-cortical loop and association cortex, where "thought" is iterative and there are no restrictions as to what patterns/knowledge get applied when.A similar idea has been explored before ("Universal Transformer", 2019), seemingly going nowhere, but perhaps "necessity is the mother of invention" and compute-starved Chinese companies are finding a way to make it work."
